# Logos

Original files from the Figma file “madita.ai 2027”, exported as SVG.

- `madita-logo-grey.svg`: wordmark in grey (#1E1E1E) with “.ai” in indigo (#343DBA) for light surfaces.
- `madita-logo-white.svg`: wordmark in white with “.ai” in indigo for grey, indigo-dark and images.
- `madita-logo-allwhite.svg`: wordmark all white, for Indigo surfaces.
- `madita-favicon-grey.svg` / `madita-favicon-white.svg`: the “m” signet for avatars and small surfaces.
- `madita-favicon-lime.svg`: the favicon, the grey signet on a lime tile.

The colours are built in (no `currentColor`), so choose the file by background.
