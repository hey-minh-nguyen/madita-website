# EmailCapture

Single-field form made of a pill input and a button, always on `surface-brand`.

- `tone="light"`: white field, interview demo (“Start interview”).
- `tone="dark"`: grey field, newsletter bar in the footer.
- Input in DM Sans Regular 15, like every form. The label is visually hidden but present (`label`).
- Optionally a `Checkbox` for consent underneath.
- The consumer provides `onSubmit(email)` and the copy.
