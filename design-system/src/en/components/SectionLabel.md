# SectionLabel

Small pill above a section headline that names the topic in two or three words (“Features”, “Field experience”, “How it works”). No full stop at the end.

- `tone="brand"`: `indigo-10` with `ink-brand`, on light surfaces.
- `tone="brand"` is 32 px high (`label-height`) with 14 px padding and 12 px type.
- `tone="accent"`: `lime` with `ink-strong`, 28 px high, on `surface-brand`.

No link, no action: it looks like a button but is text. Always sits `space-10` above the headline; usually inside a `SectionHeader` rather than on its own.
