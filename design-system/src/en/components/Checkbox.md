# Checkbox

Consent under forms; draws in `currentColor` and therefore works on white and on indigo.

- Box 16 px (Figma draws 10 px, enlarged for usability), text 12 px.
- Always with a plain-language label; never preselected for marketing consent.
- Box and tick take the text colour of the surface: `ink` on white, white on `surface-brand`, `surface-inverse` and `surface-brand-deep`. Never a dark box on a dark surface.
