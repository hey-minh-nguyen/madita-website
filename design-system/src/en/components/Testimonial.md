# Testimonial

Customer quote in `quote` (DM Sans Regular Italic 15) below a hairline, with name and role in `caption` underneath.

- Two side by side below a large quote headline (`display`/`h2` with highlight, e.g. “I love AI. I love it.”).
- Several quotes run as a carousel: change every 6 seconds, pause on hover or focus, arrows and dots stay usable.
- Quotation marks per language: German „…“, English “…”.
- Only real, approved quotes with real names; never publish placeholders.
