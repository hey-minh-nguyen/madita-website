# WidgetChart

Widget for growth: a header (“Interviews per week”) and five to seven bars rising from the bottom edge of the panel. The last two bars full `indigo`, the rest at 60% opacity.

- `values` scale freely to the panel height; the trend should rise.
- `highlight` = number of highlighted bars (default 2).
- No axes or numbers; the widget shows a trend, not statistics. Real numbers belong in `Stat`.
