# FeatureGrid

The feature row: five `FeatureCard`s of 264 × 386 with `space-4` gaps, 1384 px wide and therefore deliberately wider than `container`.

- Order as in Figma: four widget cards in lime, mint, turquoise, sky-blue, then the indigo CTA card at the end.
- Below 1440 px the row becomes horizontally scrollable with snap on every card; the page margin stays `gutter`.
- Above it sits a `SectionHeader` with `align="left"` (“What Madita can do”) and an `outline` button with arrow on the right.
